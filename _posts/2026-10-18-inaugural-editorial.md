The journal's first publication is out: the inaugural editorial, *Silent Failures in Scientific Software: On the Founding of Examen Silentii*. It sets out why the journal exists, what counts as a silent failure, and what kind of work it will publish. It's archived on Zenodo with its own DOI and listed on the [Articles](/articles/) page.

The first examination articles are next. If you've found a case where scientific software or a public dataset gets something wrong without saying so, the [submissions repository](https://github.com/examen-silentii/submissions/issues/new/choose) is open, and so is the form to volunteer as a reviewer or editor.

Noah Parsons, Founding Editor
