This page is for the maintainers of software or data examined in an ExSil article.

## Why you may have been contacted

Authors who submit to ExSil must report the problem they found to you before submitting. If your project is the subject of a submission, the handling editor will also invite you to take part in the review.

## What you can do

- **Comment during review.** You may comment on the submission issue at any point, for example to correct a factual error, explain intended behaviour or describe a fix.
- **Submit a response.** You may write a formal response to the examination. Responses that meet the journal's standards of accuracy are published alongside the article, with their own DOI. Use the *Submit a maintainer response* form in the [submissions repository](https://github.com/examen-silentii/submissions/issues/new/choose).
- **Ask for a correction after publication.** If a published article misstates the facts, contact the editors through the submissions repository. Corrections are made openly.

## Time to respond

Maintainers are given at least 30 days after the invitation to submit a response before an article is published. The editors will extend this on request where a fix or release is in progress.

## What the journal will not do

ExSil does not publish unverified claims, ridicule of projects or their maintainers, or details of a security vulnerability before the project's security policy permits.
