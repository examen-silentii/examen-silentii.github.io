This page describes what happens after an article is accepted.

## Volumes and numbering

ExSil publishes continuously. Articles are numbered in order of publication, and each calendar year forms one volume, beginning with Volume 1.

## Archiving

Each accepted article is archived on Zenodo, in the [Examen Silentii community](https://zenodo.org/communities/examen-silentii), as a journal article with:

- the final manuscript as a PDF, and its source files;
- the journal title, volume and article number;
- the CC BY 4.0 licence;
- links to the open review on GitHub and to the archived test materials.

Zenodo assigns the article its DOI, which is the permanent reference for citation.

## Citation format

> Surname, G., & Surname, G. (Year). Title of the article. *Examen Silentii*, Volume, Article N. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.0000000

## Responses

A published maintainer response is archived in the same way, with its own DOI, and linked to the article it answers in both directions.

## Corrections

A correction is published as a new version of the Zenodo record, with a dated notice describing the change. Earlier versions remain available.
